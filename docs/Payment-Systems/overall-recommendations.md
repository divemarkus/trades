# Overall Recommendations

For **international travel**, the most important factors are:

* Acceptance worldwide
* Works across airlines, hotels, restaurants, transit
* Easy to restore on a replacement phone
* Supported by most banks
* Secure
* Minimal vendor lock-in

## Overall Recommendation

### 🥇 Best Overall: Google Wallet

* ✅ Works on nearly every Android brand (Samsung, Pixel, Motorola, OnePlus, Sony, Xiaomi, etc.)
* ✅ Excellent support in North America, Europe, Australia, Japan, Singapore, and many other markets
* ✅ Stores payment cards, boarding passes, transit passes, loyalty cards, and many event tickets
* ✅ Easy to move to a new Android phone by signing into your Google account

### 🥈 Best Alternative: Apple Wallet

* ✅ Excellent international acceptance
* ✅ Often the smoothest user experience
* ✅ Deep integration with iPhone, Apple Watch, and Mac
* ❌ Requires staying in the Apple ecosystem

If you already use an iPhone and don't plan to switch, **Apple Wallet** is arguably the best experience. If you value flexibility and the ability to move between Android devices, **Google Wallet** is the stronger long-term choice.

## 1. Contactless (NFC) Symbol — The Universal Sign

```
     ))))
   ))))
 ))))
```

If you see this symbol on the terminal, it usually means you can use:

* ✅ Apple Wallet
* ✅ Google Wallet
* ✅ Samsung Wallet
* ✅ Contactless credit/debit cards

---

## 2. Payment Terminal Logo

Modern payment terminals often display:

```
Tap Here

     ))))
```

That generally indicates NFC payments are enabled.

---

## 3. Look for Wallet Logos

Many merchants display decals like:

```
 Pay

Google Wallet

Samsung Wallet
```

Even if those logos aren't present, the **contactless symbol** is the more reliable indicator.

# Practical Travel Tips

| Situation            | Recommendation                                                                                                                                                 |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Everyday purchases   | Use your mobile wallet by default.                                                                                                                             |
| International travel | Carry one physical credit card as a backup in case a terminal lacks NFC or a merchant requires chip insertion.                                                 |
| Rural areas          | Keep a physical card available; older terminals still exist in some locations.                                                                                 |
| Battery runs low     | Carry a charger or power bank, or use your backup card.                                                                                                        |
| Lost phone           | Use **Find My** (Apple) or **Find My Device** (Google) to lock or erase the device remotely, then continue using your backup card until you replace the phone. |

## Bottom Line

For someone who travels internationally and wants the broadest compatibility with minimal ecosystem lock-in:

1. 🥇 **Google Wallet** — Best overall balance of compatibility, portability, and Android ecosystem flexibility.
2. 🥈 **Apple Wallet** — Outstanding choice if you already use an iPhone and Apple Watch.
3. 🥉 **Samsung Wallet** — Excellent on Samsung devices, but its advantages diminish if you ever switch to a different Android brand.

A good travel strategy is to **load at least two different credit cards into your mobile wallet** (preferably from different payment networks, such as Visa and Mastercard or American Express where accepted) and keep **one physical card** in a separate location as an emergency backup.
