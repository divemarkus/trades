# San Jose, California

> Why is San Jose, CA amonst the safest large U.S. cities and how does it rank in the world?


San Jose is a bit of an anomaly among large U.S. cities. It combines a population of about 1 million people with crime rates that are often closer to affluent suburbs than to other major American cities.

## Why San Jose Is So Safe

### 1. Extremely High Household Incomes

San Jose is the center of Silicon Valley and has one of the highest median household incomes among major U.S. cities.

Major employers include:

* [NVIDIA](https://www.nvidia.com)
* [Adobe](https://www.adobe.com)
* [Cisco](https://www.cisco.com)
* [PayPal](https://www.paypal.com)
* [eBay](https://www.ebay.com)
* Plus many other tech giants around the area

Areas with high employment, high educational attainment, and high incomes generally experience less violent crime.

### 2. High Educational Attainment

San Jose has one of the highest percentages of college-educated residents among large U.S. cities.

Research consistently finds correlations between:

* Higher education
* Higher earnings
* Lower violent crime

### 3. Less Concentrated Poverty

Compared with cities such as:

* Chicago
* Philadelphia
* Baltimore
* Detroit

San Jose has fewer large neighborhoods with concentrated multi-generational poverty.

### 4. Suburban Urban Design

San Jose is geographically huge (469 km² / 181 sq mi) and was built largely around post-war suburban development.

Characteristics include:

* Single-family homes
* Lower population density
* Less crowding
* Fewer large public-housing concentrations

This tends to reduce some categories of violent crime.

### 5. Strong Local Economy

The Silicon Valley economy creates:

* High-paying jobs
* Venture capital investment
* Low unemployment relative to many U.S. cities

Economic opportunity tends to reduce many crime drivers.

## How Does San Jose Rank Globally?

San Jose is safe by U.S. standards, but it is **not in the same league as East Asian cities**.

### Global Large-City Safety Tier

| Tier         | Cities                                  |
| ------------ | --------------------------------------- |
| World Elite  | Tokyo, Seoul, Singapore                 |
| Very Safe    | Osaka, Taipei, Zurich                   |
| Safe         | San Jose, San Diego, Munich, Copenhagen |
| Moderate     | Seattle, London, New York City          |

## The Interesting Comparison: San Jose vs Seattle

| Metric                  |          San Jose |                   Seattle |
| ----------------------- | ----------------: | ------------------------: |
| Population              |             ~1.0M |                     ~780K |
| Metro GDP               | Higher per capita | Higher total regional GDP |
| Median Household Income |            Higher |                     Lower |
| Violent Crime           |             Lower |                    Higher |
| Property Crime          |             Lower |                    Higher |
| Tech Jobs Density       |    Extremely High |            Extremely High |
| Homelessness Visibility |             Lower |                    Higher |

San Jose's combination of wealth, education, suburban layout, and concentration of high-paying technology jobs makes it arguably **the safest city of one million people or more in the United States**. Globally, however, cities like Tokyo, Seoul, Singapore, Taipei, and several Northern European cities still outperform it on most safety metrics.
