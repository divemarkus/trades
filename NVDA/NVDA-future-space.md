### What SR-1 Actually Is

NASA's proposed **Space Reactor-1 (SR-1) Freedom** is a nuclear-electric spacecraft intended to demonstrate fission-powered propulsion and power generation on a Mars mission. It would deliver the Skyfall helicopter scouts to Mars and prove technologies needed for sustained deep-space operations. ([Wikipedia][1])

The important part is not the helicopters.

The important part is:

```text
Nuclear Reactor
       ↓
Electric Power
       ↓
Continuous Operations
       ↓
Mars Infrastructure
```

NASA is essentially testing technologies needed for permanent off-world infrastructure. ([Wikipedia][1])

Once you have reliable power, AI becomes the next layer; once you have AI, large-scale autonomous settlements become much more realistic. ([Wikipedia][1])
