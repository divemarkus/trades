
# CLARITY Act

## CLARITY Act — brief background and current status

The **Digital Asset Market CLARITY Act (H.R. 3633)** is the major U.S. crypto **market-structure bill**. Its purpose is to replace the current patchwork of SEC/CFTC interpretations with statutory rules.

### What it would do

* Define which digital assets fall under **SEC securities regulation** versus **CFTC commodity oversight**.
* Create federal registration and operating rules for crypto exchanges, brokers, custodians, and other intermediaries.
* Clarify treatment of secondary-market trading and decentralized networks.
* Add customer-protection, disclosure, and market-structure requirements.
* The Senate's evolving version also addresses issues around developer protections, illicit finance, banking/stablecoin concerns, and ethics restrictions for government officials. ([Congress.gov][1])

### Legislative status — as of August 27, 2026

| Stage                    | Status                                                         |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| House of Representatives | **Passed 294–134** in July 2025                                |
| Senate Banking Committee | **Passed 15–9** in May 2026                                    |
| Senate Agriculture work  | Incorporated into broader Senate market-structure negotiations |
| Senate floor vote        | **Not yet passed; delayed until at least September**           |
| President                | Has not received final legislation                             |

The Senate released an updated merged draft in July, but the bill subsequently lost momentum and the Senate postponed floor action until at least September. The main unresolved issues include ethics/conflict-of-interest provisions, anti-money-laundering and law-enforcement rules, banking-sector concerns, and obtaining enough bipartisan votes. ([The Block][2])

## My probability assessment

**I would no longer say passage in 2026 is the base case.**

My estimate:

| Senate makes progress but final passage slips into 2027 |     **45%** |
| Materially stalls / requires major rewrite              |     **25%** |

The positive: the House vote was strongly bipartisan, and the Senate Banking Committee also produced bipartisan support. There is substantial political and industry pressure to create a U.S. market-structure framework. ([Senator Cynthia Lummis][3])

The negative: **the Senate is the bottleneck**. Reuters reported that the bill has been pushed to at least September, and the approaching midterm election makes legislative time increasingly scarce. ([Reuters][4])

### Bottom line

**CLARITY is politically alive, but it is not close enough to call “inevitable.”** The key catalyst is the expected September Senate action. If Senate leadership can get a procedural vote and demonstrate a viable bipartisan coalition, odds could rapidly improve. If September passes without meaningful movement, I would expect the market to start pricing the legislation as a **2027 story instead**.

For crypto investors, I view eventual market-structure legislation as **highly likely over the longer term**; the real uncertainty is **when**, and whether the current CLARITY Act survives largely intact.
