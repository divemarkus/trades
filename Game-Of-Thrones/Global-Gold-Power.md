# The Most Important Relationship

Most people think:

```text
Gold vs Dollar
```

The reality is:

```text
Gold + Dollar
```

because central banks increasingly hold both.

### Why?

Gold provides:

✔ Trust

✔ Neutrality

✔ Protection

The dollar provides:

✔ Liquidity

✔ Commerce

✔ Global settlement

Countries need both.


The key takeaway is that **gold is not replacing the dollar**. Instead, gold is increasingly becoming the **trust layer**, while the dollar and stablecoins remain the **transaction layer**. China is accumulating the trust layer; the United States still controls most of the transaction layer. The next decade will likely determine how much power shifts between the two.
