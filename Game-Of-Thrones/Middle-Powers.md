# Middle Powers (rest of the countries)

> Are there any countries prepared for this scenario of global money shift, like for example Indonesia with QRIS?

Yes. In fact, one of the biggest misconceptions is that only the U.S., China, and BRICS are preparing.

Many middle powers are quietly building the infrastructure for a more fragmented monetary world.

Indonesia is actually a very interesting example.

---

# The Three Strategies Countries Are Pursuing

Countries are generally choosing one of three paths:

| Strategy           | Objective                                   | Examples                            |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Dollar System      | Stay integrated with U.S. financial markets | Japan, South Korea, Singapore       |
| Alternative System | Reduce dollar dependence                    | China, Russia, Iran                 |
| Hybrid System      | Use both worlds                             | Indonesia, India, UAE, Saudi Arabia |

The hybrid strategy may prove to be the smartest.

---

# Indonesia's Strategy

## QRIS

Indonesia developed QRIS.

Most people think QRIS is simply a payment app.

It's much bigger than that.

QRIS creates:

```text
Consumer
    │
    ▼
QRIS
    │
    ▼
Local Bank
    │
    ▼
Local Currency
```

The important part:

A domestic payment network means less dependence on:

* Visa
* Mastercard
* SWIFT-linked payment chains

---

## Why Indonesia Built It

Indonesia learned from:

* Asian Financial Crisis (1997)
* Global Financial Crisis (2008)
* Sanctions examples around the world

The lesson:

> Financial sovereignty matters.

Indonesia wants the ability to keep domestic commerce running regardless of geopolitical events.

---

# Indonesia's Bigger Plan

Indonesia has been quietly working on:

### Local Currency Settlement (LCS)

With countries including:

* Malaysia
* Thailand
* Japan
* China

Instead of:

```text
Rupiah
   ↓
USD
   ↓
Baht
```

They want:

```text
Rupiah
   ↓
Baht
```

This reduces transaction costs and dollar dependence.

# India May Be The Most Prepared Country

If I had to pick one country outside the U.S. and China, it would be India.

Why?

India is building:

### UPI

Unified Payments Interface

which has become one of the most successful payment systems in history.

```text
Citizen
    │
    ▼
UPI
    │
    ▼
Bank
```

India can process billions of transactions monthly.

# Assessment of Indonesia

Indonesia is not trying to become the next reserve currency issuer.

Instead, Indonesia is pursuing something arguably more realistic:

> Build enough domestic payment and settlement infrastructure so that Indonesia can function whether the future is dominated by dollars, yuan, gold-backed trade, stablecoins, or some combination of all four.

That is a very pragmatic strategy, and among major emerging economies, Indonesia is arguably further ahead than many people realize because QRIS, local-currency settlement initiatives, and its large domestic market give it more financial resilience than countries that depend entirely on foreign payment networks.
