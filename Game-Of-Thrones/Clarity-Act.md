# What Is The Federal Reserve Doing?

The Fed is fighting two battles simultaneously.

## Battle #1: Inflation

The Fed must ensure inflation remains under control.

If inflation returns:

```text
Rates Stay Higher
```

If inflation falls:

```text
Rates Can Fall
```

---

## Battle #2: Debt Sustainability

This is the bigger issue.

Federal debt continues growing.

Higher rates mean:

* Higher interest expenses
* Larger deficits
* More Treasury issuance

The Fed knows this.

## The One Thing Most Investors Miss

The U.S. is not trying to stop the future.

It is trying to **own the rails of the future**.

China is accumulating hard assets (gold, commodities, manufacturing).

The U.S. is building the financial infrastructure (Treasuries, capital markets, AI, stablecoins, payment rails).

The winner may not be the country with the most gold.

The winner may be the country whose financial network everyone still uses every day.
