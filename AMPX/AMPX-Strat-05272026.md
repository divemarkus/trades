# 1) Major Bear Market

## Probability: Moderate-to-High

Reasons:

* elevated valuations across growth
* weakening consumer/industrial signals
* geopolitical instability
* debt/refinancing stress
* possible policy uncertainty

---

## Fed Chair / Rates Impact

If future Fed leadership becomes more dovish:

* rates fall
* liquidity rises

That can help speculative growth stocks eventually.

BUT…

### Initial rate cuts during recession are often bearish first.

Example:

* 2001
* 2008
* 2020 early phase

Markets usually panic BEFORE stimulus works.

---

## Effect on AMPX

Small-cap growth names often fall:

* 50–80%
  during real bear markets.

That is normal historically.

---

# 2) Dilution Event

## Probability: HIGH

This is the single biggest realistic risk.

AMPX still needs:

* manufacturing scale
* capacity expansion
* working capital
* customer execution

Growth companies commonly issue:

* shares
* convertibles
* ATM offerings

---

## Important Nuance

Dilution is NOT always bad.

If they raise capital to:

* expand production
* secure contracts
* improve margins

…it may actually strengthen long-term value.

---

## But Market Reaction?

Usually:

* short-term bearish

Especially after huge rallies.
